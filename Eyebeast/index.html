Clinical Recommendations

20min

The 20-20-20 Rule

Every 20 minutes, look at an object 20 feet away for 20 seconds to relax your ciliary muscle and reduce digital eye strain.

20in

Optimal Monitor Distance

Keep your monitor 20–26 inches (50–65 cm) from your eyes. The centre of the screen should be 10–15° below eye level.

15bpm

Normal Blink Rate

A healthy blink rate is 15–20 blinks per minute. Screen use can reduce this to 5–7 BPM, causing dryness, fatigue, and blurred vision.

Welcome to Eyebeast

Your private, real-time eye health companion

1

Allow Camera Access

Click Allow when your browser asks for webcam permission. The camera feed never leaves your device — all processing is 100% local.

2

Position Your Face

Sit 50–70 cm from your screen. Keep your face centred in the small camera preview. Good lighting helps — avoid strong backlight.

3

Watch Your Metrics

Eyebeast tracks your Blink Rate (BPM) and Eye Strain Risk in real time. A healthy blink rate is 15–20 BPM. Below 15 BPM is when eye strain sets in.

4

Take Breaks & Do Exercises

A 20-20-20 break reminder fires every 20 minutes. Visit the 💪 Eye Exercises page for guided exercises to reduce strain.

No data is stored, transmitted, or shared. Everything runs in your browser.
